Affiliate Disclosure

Last Updated: March 22, 2026

01. General Disclosure

At PickyCoupon, we believe in transparency and honesty. We want to be upfront about how we maintain this platform and provide high-quality, handpicked deals to our users for free.

Some of the links on PickyCoupon are "affiliate links." This means if you click on the link and purchase an item or sign up for a service, we may receive an affiliate commission at no extra cost to you. In fact, using our links often provides you with exclusive discounts and verified promo codes that aren't available elsewhere.

02. Our Relationship with Merchants

PickyCoupon is a participant in various affiliate marketing programs, including but not limited to Amazon Associates, Impact, CJ Affiliate, Rakuten, and direct partnership programs with brands using platforms like Social Snowball and Shopify.

These partnerships allow us to earn a small commission which supports the operational costs of our website, including hosting, development, and our team of deal hunters who verify every coupon manually.

03. Editorial Integrity & "Picky" Standard

Our "Picky" philosophy means we prioritize quality over quantity. The compensation received will never influence the content, topics, or posts made on this website.

We only feature brands and products that we believe provide value to our community. All coupons and deals are verified to the best of our ability before being published.

04. No Extra Cost to You

Clicking an affiliate link through PickyCoupon does NOT increase the price you pay. The commission is paid by the retailer as a "thank you" for referring a customer to them. Sometimes, these links actually unlock even deeper discounts than what you would find by going directly to the merchant's site.
